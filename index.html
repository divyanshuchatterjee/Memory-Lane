The issue happens because the `side` layout parameter (`left` or `right`) is saved as static data inside Firebase whenever a new stop is created. When a stop in the middle is deleted, the remaining items keep their original `side` properties, creating situations where two "left" or two "right" cards end up next to each other. To fix this, we should **decouple the side logic from Firebase**. Instead of saving `stop.side` to the database, we can determine the side dynamically in the frontend loop as the stops are rendered.
